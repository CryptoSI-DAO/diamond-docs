# Introduction 💎

> *Paper hands fund diamond hands. On-chain. Forever.*

**Diamond Hands Protocol (DHP) is a fully deployed, admin-free vault protocol that converts trading friction into holder rewards.** Every vault wraps one ERC-20 token. Entering costs a fixed **5%** tax; leaving costs a fixed **10%**. Of every tax collected:

- **80%** is distributed pro-rata to remaining holders, in the underlying token
- **10%** is permanently locked out of supply (burn-by-accounting)
- **10%** funds the DAO and the vault's creator and platform partners

There is no yield source, no treasury strategy and no team payoff — value flows from churn to conviction, mechanically, on-chain.

## The problem & the thesis

Token communities leak value through churn. Every panic-sell dumps on the holders who stayed; every "reward" pool eventually gets farmed and deserted. DHP makes the churn itself fund the conviction.

| The problem | The DHP answer |
|---|---|
| **Exit asymmetry** — sellers exit freely while holders absorb the dump | **Tax the exit, not the victim** — a fixed 10% leave-tax is paid by whoever leaves, into a pool owned by whoever stays |
| **Value extraction** — team taxes ship value out | **Rewards in kind** — dividends are paid in the underlying token; no second token to dump, no inflation |
| **Admin risk** — mutable taxes, pause keys, upgrade keys | **Remove the admin** — no pause, no setter, no upgrade; the factory is renounced. The code is the policy |
| **Fake yield** — APYs that are dilution or marketing budget | **Burn by accounting** — 10% of every tax is locked out of backing forever; no yield is promised because none exists |

## What DHP is — and is not

| Diamond Hands Protocol **IS** | Diamond Hands Protocol is **NOT** |
|---|---|
| A permissionless vault factory: any wallet can create a vault for any qualifying ERC-20 | A yield protocol — there is no external yield source of any kind |
| A dividend engine: taxes accrue pro-rata to holders, claimable in the underlying token | An investment product or a promise of profit — payouts depend entirely on future churn |
| A deflationary mechanism: every tax permanently locks 10% of itself out of backing | A trading protocol — vaults never swap, stake, lend or touch your tokens beyond holding them |
| A key-less system: vaults expose zero admin functions; factory ownership is renounced | Governed by a team — there are no team keys, no treasury control over vaults, no upgrade path |

:::tip Design axiom
Everything a future "bad actor" could want to do — raise taxes, redirect dividends, pause withdrawals, mint shares, redirect the burn — is not merely forbidden; **there is no function for it**. The attack surface is deleted, not protected.
:::
